EGGSCAPE · PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · English

Eggscape uses Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics for usage measurement. The current code includes Apple's tracking permission request; we have not verified an in-app Google UMP consent flow. These are distinct controls.

1. Controller and contact

Jorge Dávila, operating under the JD Apps brand, is responsible for the developer-controlled processing described here. Privacy and data rights enquiries: support@playjdapps.com.

2. Game data on your device

Eggscape stores game-related progress and preferences locally to operate the game. Deleting the app generally removes locally stored app data, subject to Apple-managed backups. The developer does not operate a separate Eggscape player account service.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Eggscape includes Google AdMob interstitial and rewarded advertising. Google and advertising partners may process IP address, device and app information, permitted identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and reliability. IP-derived approximate location may be processed. Advertising can involve personal data even when an ad is non-personalized.

The app contains a request for Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. ATT controls tracking permission under Apple's rules; it is not equivalent to consent for all advertising storage and processing under EU privacy rules. The reviewed code did not establish an integrated Google UMP consent flow. We are reviewing the consent implementation and do not claim here that a UMP choice screen is available.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google services on partner apps.

4. Analytics — Firebase

Eggscape includes Google Analytics for Firebase to measure game use and performance. Depending on configuration and permissions, Firebase may process app events, device/app information, diagnostic data and app-instance identifiers. The reviewed code shows analytics initialization and event recording; it does not establish an opt-in setting equivalent to Pulse Loop's. Do not assume that refusing ATT disables Firebase Analytics.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

Local game data is used to deliver requested game features. Support communications are processed to respond to your request. Advertising and analytics processing must have an appropriate legal basis and, where required, prior consent for device storage/access and related processing. The current implementation requires a separate compliance review for users in the EEA/UK/Switzerland. This policy does not replace a legally required consent mechanism.

6. Support enquiries

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to investigate and respond to your request. Email is handled through our professional email provider, Arsys. Please avoid including sensitive personal data.

7. Recipients and international transfers

Google provides advertising and analytics services; Apple provides app distribution and device services; Arsys provides email; and GitHub Pages hosts this website. Service providers may process information outside your country subject to applicable safeguards and their own terms. See Apple Privacy and GitHub Privacy.

8. Retention and controls

Local game data remains while retained on your device, unless deleted or reset. Support messages are kept for the period needed to handle enquiries and applicable obligations. Google and Apple determine retention of records processed in their services according to their settings and policies. Deleting the game does not automatically erase data already held by third parties. You can review Apple's tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, and withdraw consent where relevant. Contact support@playjdapps.com. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

10. Children

We do not intentionally request sensitive information about children through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can assess the request. Age suitability and advertising settings must also be checked against the released build and store declarations.

11. Changes

We update this policy when data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. For questions contact support@playjdapps.com.

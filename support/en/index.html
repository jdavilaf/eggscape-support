Support

Need help with Eggscape? Find answers below or email our support team.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress and scores are stored locally. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot before considering possible loss of local data. JD Apps does not operate a player account service for synchronizing Eggscape progress.

An ad or reward is unavailable.

Check your connection and try later. Ad availability depends on the provider and privacy choices. A reward requires the qualifying ad completion; an unavailable or interrupted ad does not guarantee a reward. Contact support if a completed rewarded ad did not grant it.

How do I change advertising choices?

In Eggscape 1.0.1, open Settings → Privacy Choices when this entry is required and shown by Google UMP. Rejecting personalization does not necessarily remove all ads. Earlier versions may have different controls.

Can I change sound and vibration?

Open Settings to adjust sound effects, music and haptic feedback on supported devices.

How do I report a privacy concern?

Email support@playjdapps.com. See the privacy policy for information about data use, retention and your rights.

Contact support

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, steps to reproduce the issue and what you expected to happen. A screenshot may help; remove unnecessary personal information. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Contact supportsupport@playjdapps.com

We reply by email. Response times vary; no fixed response time is guaranteed.
